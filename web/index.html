Why EM fails

Prior-shift EM (Saerens–Latinne–Decaestecker) assumes the source classifier is well calibrated on the target. Generator shift breaks exactly that assumption. EM converges — confidently — to the wrong phishing prior, and the estimate does not move whether you give it 5 iterations or 200.
